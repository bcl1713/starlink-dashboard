## Review Focus

- Concurrent customer connection and Close: authoritative backend rejects unsafe close transactionally.
- Inline conference accepted but response lost: retain both drafts and reconcile before selecting or retrying.
- Old established-without-verification history: never silently fabricate a completed check.
- Missing/stale integrated timing or ad hoc transport: unknown, never all-clear; PoP remains advisory.
- Overnight/backdated note or parked editor: date/time stable independently of locale and retry clock.

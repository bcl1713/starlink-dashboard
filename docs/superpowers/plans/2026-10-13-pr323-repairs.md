**Goal:** Resolve issues 324, 325, and 326 on existing PR323 and prove the

renderer.

[Approved design](../specs/2026-10-13-pr323-operator-and-export-repair.md).

## Global Constraints

- Work only in the existing `feat/itinerary-xband-planning` worktree and PR323.
  Preserve the primary `dev` checkout; no merge or deployment is authorized.
- Follow the approved spec and existing itinerary policy/resource contracts.
  Flight level is the visible default for unlabelled itinerary AR altitudes.
- Preserve tracked Python 3.11, the frontend Node engine constraint, and locked
  dependencies; these changes need no additional product dependency.
- Preserve source dates, occurrence identity, canonical evaluations, package
  schema, explicit executable-plan approval, and inactive installed legs.
- Keep real operational artifacts private. Do not overwrite the retained
  baseline evidence or use synthetic inputs as final UX/map acceptance.
- ZIP blocks are 64 KiB, spooling starts at 8 MiB, ready packages expire after
  30 minutes, renderer jobs are bounded at 120 seconds, and aggregate export
  preparation is bounded at 600 seconds from the request.
- Health/status/mission probes have a two-second deadline on the named Forge
  configuration. Target at most 20 explicit real-workflow actions under the
  retained original counting convention; Brian must accept residual exceptions.
- Before every resource-owning command, record handles, PIDs/groups, paths,
  projects, and private volumes. Use wall-clock timeout plus kill grace and
  exit/signal cleanup. Verify teardown immediately after checks.

## Review Focus

- A save during export must not produce a ZIP combining two input generations;
  owned ready downloads must remain consistently labeled immutable snapshots.
- A late browser preview or another operator's write must not erase local edits
  or let stale approvals publish executable legs.
- Old packages without altitude provenance must retain their saved
  interpretation; the new FL default applies to new eligible source rows, not
  historical plans.
- Repeated coordinates, sparse timestamps, and minute precision must retain
  canonical occurrence/window relationships and useful supported maps.
- Timeout, restart, or browser cancellation must release task-owned workers and
  files without killing another export's shared preparation or prior evidence.

# Timeline and route mapping

Map leg timeline intervals against the leg route and effective timing. Keep
separate crossings and repeated occurrences distinct; do not infer timing by
coordinate identity alone. Missing timing or unsupported geometry needs an
explicit diagnostic. Use the production exporter helpers rather than copying the
retired illustrative flat-mission snippets. See
[`map_inputs.py`](../../../backend/starlink-location/app/mission/exporter/map_inputs.py)
and
[manual AR route estimates](../planning/manual-ar-derived-route-estimates.md).
